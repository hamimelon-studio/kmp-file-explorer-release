## Common questions

**Which NAS works with Melon?**
Any NAS or network drive that speaks SMB or WebDAV, for example Synology, QNAP, UGREEN, TrueNAS, or a USB disk shared by your router.

**When does Melon back up?**
By default only at home on Wi-Fi, when your NAS answers on your home network. It never uses mobile data unless you allow it.

**Will Melon delete anything on my NAS?**
No. Melon only adds files. It never deletes or overwrites anything on your NAS, and it does not upload the same photo twice, even after you reinstall it or move to a new phone.

**Do my photos go to a cloud?**
No. Photos go straight from your phone to your own NAS. See the [privacy policy](privacy.md).
